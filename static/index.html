Book Title

Point your iPhone, iPad, Android, or tablet camera at the QR code to download directly into Apple Books, Moon+ Reader, or Kindle App over your home Wi-Fi!

✉️ Email Directly to Your Kindle

Amazon assigns every Kindle e-reader and Kindle app a unique email address (like yourname@kindle.com). Sending your book file to this email automatically delivers it to your Kindle device!

📖 Open Publication Distribution System (OPDS)

E-reader apps like KOReader, Moon+ Reader, Marvin, Aldiko, and PocketBook can browse your entire personal catalog and download books directly over your local Wi-Fi without any cables!

💡 In KOReader or Moon+ Reader: Go to Net Library → Add OPDS Catalog, paste this URL, and your whole library will be browsable right on your e-reader!

⚙️ Device & Kindle Email Settings

Find this in your Amazon account under Manage Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings.
⚠️ Important Amazon Step: In your Amazon account under Personal Document Settings, make sure to add this sender email to your "Approved Personal Document E-mail List", otherwise Amazon will block the incoming book!

📥 Direct Upload

Paste any direct download link to an .epub, .pdf, .m4b, or .mp3 file. The server will download the file, clean site watermarks, and fetch metadata and covers from Google Books, Goodreads, or Audible automatically.
⚡ For OceanofPDF & AudiobookBay:
Because sites like OceanofPDF use Cloudflare anti-bot verification and AudiobookBay uses BitTorrent client swarms, download or torrent your files to the folder below. Then click "Scan & Import Drop Folder" — the server automatically cleans watermarks (like oceanofpdf.com_... or [AudioBookBay.lu]), pulls official Goodreads/Audible covers, and imports them!
Tip: Set this folder as your Chrome/Edge download directory or your qBittorrent default save path.

Option A: Scan Calibre Library Folder

Enter the folder path where your Calibre Library is stored (contains metadata.db and author subfolders). Works with local paths, mapped drives (e.g. Z:\Calibre), or network/NAS UNC paths (e.g. \\NAS\books\Calibre)! Ebooks, series, tags, ratings, and covers will be imported automatically.

Option B: Upload Calibre metadata.db

If your Calibre library is on another device, drag & drop your metadata.db file here to import your full book catalog, series, and ratings as TBR/Wishlist.
